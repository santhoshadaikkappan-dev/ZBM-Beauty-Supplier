Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our unbranded sample kits, custom logo branding, and USA manufacturing compliance.

What is included in each $99 USD Discovery Kit?

Every Discovery Kit includes 7 to 10 physical, pre-filled unbranded laboratory formulations in luxury cosmetic containers (frosted droppers, glass jars, PCR tubes). You also receive a complete INCI ingredient breakdown, batch Certificate of Analysis (COA), and an official turnkey B2B quotation for scaling from 50 to 1,000+ units with your logo.

Can I rebrand these formulations with my own logo and brand packaging?

Absolutely. ZANDRA BEAUTY MATRIX (ZBM) is a turnkey private label manufacturer. You can use our interactive 3D Brand Studio to preview your logo in real time. When you place a production order, our factory handles full silkscreen printing, gold foil hot stamping, custom carton packaging, and tamper-evident shrink sealing.

Are ZBM formulations certified and compliant for sale in the USA?

Yes. All products are manufactured in cGMP 21 CFR Part 700/701 compliant, US FDA registered, and WHO-GMP Grade-D cleanroom sterile facilities. Formulations comply with USA cosmetics safety standards, are cruelty-free, and come with safety dossiers ready for retail distribution.

What are the minimum order quantities (MOQ) for bulk production?

Sample kits have ZERO MOQ (you can purchase a single discovery kit for $99). When you are ready to scale with your custom brand, our startup tier begins at just 50 units (20% off), 250 units (35% off), 500 units (50% off), and 1,000+ units (65% off).

What is the shipping cost and delivery timeframe?

Sample kit shipping is a flat $29.00 USD worldwide via insured express air courier (DHL Express / FedEx Priority). Delivery to addresses in the USA, Canada, and Europe typically arrives within 4 to 6 business days with door-to-door tracking.

What payment methods are supported?

We accept instant online checkout via PayPal, Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover. For wholesale and corporate B2B transactions, we also accept USA Domestic Bank ACH / Fedwire direct to our Citi Bank USA commercial account with zero processing fees.
